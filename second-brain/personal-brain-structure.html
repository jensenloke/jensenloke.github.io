Personal Brain Structure

What my personal second brain actually looks like on disk: one numbered PARA-style vault, enumerated page by page and redacted for publication.

Jensen Loke · 30 September 2026 · snapshot · ~5 min read

This is a dated structural snapshot of the personal brain described in The Last Project You Build Alone and Two Brains, One Operator. Folder contents are genericized; page counts are real, enumerated over MCP on 30 September 2026. The company half has its own structure reference.

What each root is for

00–02 · Capture flow

Dashboards, one append-here Inbox, dated daily summaries. Human writes; the agent files.

10-domains · Life areas

Ongoing responsibilities with no end date: work, family, home, health, finance, learning, travel, career.

20-projects · Engagements

Time-boxed work under clients/<client>, personal/, infra/, completed/. “Active” is a frontmatter status, never a folder.

30-knowledge · Reference

Entities filed by kind: people, companies, concepts, places, devices, agents, investments, meetings, research, runbooks, sources.

40-outputs · Artefacts

Generated reports and slides. The archived agent-ops dailies stay in place; that series is retired.

50-portfolio · Published

Mirrors this website 1:1 — one site entry corresponds to one canonical brain page.

90-system · Rules

Policies, agent skills and their overrides, templates, indexes, and the state-of-the-brain page agents read before filing anything.

Flat roots · Dissolved

Loose roots like people/, concepts/, originals/, inbox/ were folded into the numbered roots in a restructure. They survive as redirects and capture residue.

Filing rule

Every page goes by primary subject, not by format or by where it came from. A PDF is not “sources material”; an email is not “inbox material”. Unsure → dated page in 01-inbox/, never a new top-level root.

How to read it without misreading it

Alias stubs are a feature. Every move in the restructure left a type: alias page at the old slug so existing wikilinks and page reads still resolve. 290 of 954 pages are stubs; indexes exclude them with type != "alias". It was a deliberate decision to keep them: nothing was deleted, and the graph stayed whole through a 139-page move.

Company knowledge is stubbed, not stored. The company-product pointer pages are references, not copies. Canonical company product, engineering, and support knowledge lives in the separate company brain. Two brains, no fallback, no dual-write - the boundary argued in Two Brains, One Operator.

Two residues are open, and the tree says so. A batch of dated hash-slugged notes from a server-side capture tool (since banned client-side), and one live page stranded on a dissolved root. Both are tracked in the brain’s own tuning backlog. Publishing the imperfections is the point: a structure page that hides its residue is marketing, not a snapshot.

Remote-only by policy. The vault lives on an external volume on a dedicated host, git-backed, and is reached through MCP only. Client devices do not run a local store, and every write is confirmed by a write-through receipt under that volume path plus a full read-back.
